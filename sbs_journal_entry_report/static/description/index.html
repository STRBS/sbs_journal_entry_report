What it adds

  • A Journal Entry Landscape report in the Print menu of journal entries, vendor bills and invoices – from the form, or for several entries selected in the list (one page per entry).
  • Account, label, partner, analytic distribution and tax names on the same line as the debit and credit. The partner, analytic, tax and foreign-currency columns appear only when the entry uses them, so a simple entry stays narrow.
  • Lines spread over several analytic plans print every account, e.g. Administration / Olaya Tower Fit-out: 60%.
  • Debit and credit totals are the sums of their columns, formatted in the company currency. Draft and cancelled entries say so in the title.
  • Internal note, “Printed by” and a signature line for the reviewer.
  • A note in the chatter when an entry is cancelled.

The report, on a real entry

A rent accrual split across two analytic plans, printed from Odoo 20.0.

Arabic, right-to-left

Printed from an Arabic database, the page runs right-to-left while amounts, entry numbers and Latin text keep their left-to-right order. Arabic labels ship with the module.

No configuration

Install it and the report is in the Print menu. It depends on Accounting (account) only and works on Community and Enterprise, on Odoo.sh and on-premise. Third-party apps cannot be installed on Odoo Online (SaaS).

The PDF uses its own landscape A4 paper format; the company’s default paper format is not changed.

New in the Odoo 20.0 version

  • Totals were the entry’s signed total, which printed negative on vendor bills and refunds; they are now the column sums.
  • Debit, credit and amount-in-currency are formatted with their currency instead of printed as raw numbers.
  • Lines spread over several analytic plans no longer stop the report with an error; invoice section and note lines are skipped.
  • Arabic translation and a right-to-left layout for Arabic databases.
  • Printing many entries at once reads their data in a fixed number of queries.
